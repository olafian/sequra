Sequra
Retirement Sequence Simulator
Sequra helps you understand retirement planning through the lens of historical market sequences. Rather than using fixed return assumptions, it runs your plan through every historical market sequence since 1871 — letting you see not just whether you survive, but how the ride feels.
Built for the FIRE community and anyone serious about understanding sequence-of-returns risk. Free, open source, no accounts required.
Data Sources
📊 Shiller US Equity Returns — real total returns 1871–2023
📈 Shiller 10-yr Treasury Returns — real bond returns 1871–2023
💵 T-bill Cash Rate — fixed 0.5% real (historical average)
Source: Robert Shiller, Yale University · econ.yale.edu/~shiller/data.htm
How it works
· Accumulation — projects portfolio growth using your return rate assumption
· Withdrawal — stress-tests your retirement portfolio against 124 historical sequences
· Sequence Explorer — click any bar in the SORR chart to see what actually happened in that era
Disclaimer
Not financial advice. Sequra is an educational tool only. Nothing here constitutes financial, investment, tax, or retirement planning advice. Consult a qualified financial advisor before making any financial decisions.
Past performance. Historical market returns do not guarantee future results. The simulations shown are based on US equity and bond market data from 1871–2023 and may not reflect future market conditions.
Simplifications. The model uses flat real spending, simplified tax treatment, and fixed cash returns. It does not account for Social Security inflation adjustments, healthcare costs, variable spending, or tax-advantaged account structures beyond what is entered.
No data collection. Sequra runs entirely in your browser. No personal data is transmitted or stored anywhere.
